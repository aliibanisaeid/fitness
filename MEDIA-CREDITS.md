# Media and source credits

## Original campaign images

`hero.jpg`, `mobility.jpg` and `strength.jpg` were generated for this website using GPT image generation. Each image depicts an adult male athlete in a bright training studio. They are illustrative campaign imagery and do not identify real clinical staff. JPEG versions preserve the generated composition while reducing transfer size. Original PNGs remain in the Codex task's working files.

## Additional campaign and device images

`shoulder.jpg`, `balance.jpg` and `active-life.jpg` were generated for this site with GPT image generation. They show, respectively, an adult man using a resistance band, an adult man stepping onto a low exercise platform, and a mature man walking outdoors. All are male-only. The JPEG files are 1200 × 800 and were optimized from the original PNGs.

`iphone-18-pro-back.png` is a generated transparent burgundy rear-device image, guided by the official [iPhone 18 Pro product announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-debuts-iphone-18-pro-and-iphone-18-pro-max/). The reference was inspected before generation. The front screen, frame, device controls and plan-creation flow are original HTML/CSS/React, with actual working inputs. Apple website photos and films were not copied into this site.

## Video

`training.mp4` is a 23.9-second local H.264 encode of **Stretching arms**, from [Coverr](https://coverr.co/videos/stretching-arms-krykrklfjv). It depicts one man stretching outdoors throughout the clip. The source was checked at one-second intervals across its full duration. The file was compressed for web playback; no speech or soundtrack is present. `training-poster.jpg` is a frame from the same clip.

Source asset: `https://cdn.coverr.co/videos/coverr-stretching-arms-9995/720p.mp4`

`strength-film.mp4` is a 26.9-second local H.264 encode of [Lifting weights](https://coverr.co/videos/lifting-weights-iksjzezrhp), showing one adult man lifting a dumbbell at the gym. This is the default cinematic scene. `strength-film-poster.jpg` comes from the same film.

`home-film.mp4` is a 14.3-second local H.264 encode of [An old man working out](https://coverr.co/videos/an-old-man-working-out-0x4mfzah6l), showing one older man exercising with dumbbells at home. A slight brightness adjustment improves visibility. `home-film-poster.jpg` comes from the same film.

Both additional clips were downloaded using Coverr's own download controls, inspected throughout their full timelines at one-second intervals, and encoded to 1280 × 720 with fast-start playback. They contain men only, without speech or a soundtrack.

[Coverr's license](https://coverr.co/license) permits free commercial and noncommercial use. This asset is used within this specific fitness website. It is not being offered as a standalone stock asset or reusable website template.
